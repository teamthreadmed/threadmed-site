Professional profile

Quadruple board-certified physician (Internal Medicine; Endocrinology, Diabetes & Metabolism; Obesity Medicine; Clinical Lipidology) with additional certifications in diabetes education (CDCES) and clinical densitometry (CCD). Practicing internal medicine and endocrinology since 1997, with an added focus on obesity and lifestyle medicine since 2017, across the endocrine, diabetes, cardiometabolic, bone, and weight-regulation axes. Former Northwestern Medicine Lake Forest Hospital endocrine and metabolic medicine director, with extensive translational experience in health-system program design, clinical operations redesign, and quality improvement. Currently in endocrine practice at True Health (Deerfield, IL). Experienced educator and medicolegal consultant for plaintiff and defense.
