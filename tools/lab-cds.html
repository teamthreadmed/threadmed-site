How this is structured

One analyte catalog, three views onto it. The build spec is written at the top of this file and every build is checked against it.
Layer 1
Order
Every lab, grouped into the practice's own sections. Checked boxes build the order list here. Draw conditions for the current selection are pinned to this page. Condition chips narrow a section without leaving it.
Layer 2
Explain
Per-test and per-group explanation with sourced thresholds, long-form modules where the number misleads, and topics that belong to no single test. Ticked tests build the handout. No individualised interpretation.
Layer 3
Decide
Condition cards, clusters, interpretation patterns, workups, interference detail, patient context — and your own decision rules, authored here and carried into the chart note.

Scope and limits. Laboratory ordering, pre-analytic conditions, and result interpretation. This tool does not support drug selection, dosing, or titration — where a result implies treatment, it names the decision and stops there. This tool supports ordering decisions; it does not replace them. Thresholds are encoded from the sources named on each card and were verified current as of August 2026 — reconfirm before acting on any borderline decision. Reference intervals are assay- and laboratory-specific and are deliberately not printed here. Units are US conventional throughout to match EPIC. Billing codes are intentionally omitted pending reconciliation against your fee schedule.
