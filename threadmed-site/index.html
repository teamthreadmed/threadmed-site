About

Why this exists

Guidelines answer questions about populations. Clinics see individuals. Most of the difficult work in medicine happens in that gap — and almost nobody writes down how it is actually done.

Thread Medicine is applied systems biology held to an evidence standard, delivered by a team and fitted to a person. Anthony Pick is a board-certified endocrinologist (internal medicine, endocrinology, obesity medicine, clinical lipidology) with certification in clinical densitometry and diabetes care and education, and a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner.
